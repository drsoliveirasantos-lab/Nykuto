## Professional reference sources

Reviewed 5 September 2026. These practitioner/manufacturer guides informed the
descriptions and image briefs; their photographs were not copied or published.

| Display label | Mechanism and intended visual distinction | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lash lift | Reshapes existing natural lashes upward; no added extension fibers. Keep natural density and separated fine lashes. | [Nouveau Lashes — LVL lash lift](https://nouveaulashes.com/treatment/lvl-lash-lift/) |
| Extensão clássica | One extension per natural lash, adding length and definition with clearly separated individual tips. | [Nouveau Lashes — classic versus hybrid](https://nouveaulashes.com/treatment/hybrid-lashes/) |
| Extensão híbrida | Individual extensions interspersed with volume fans; a textured intermediate look. | [Nouveau Lashes — hybrid](https://nouveaulashes.com/treatment/hybrid-lashes/) |
| Volume russo | Fans of fine extensions give a fuller, denser silhouette; the example intentionally shows a full-volume result. | [London Lash — Russian volume](https://londonlashpro.com/blogs/news/the-complete-guide-to-russian-lashes) |
| Efeito gatinho (cat-eye) | Longer extensions toward the outer region create an elongated shape. This is a mapping/styling effect that can be combined with extension methods, not a separate lifting procedure. | [London Lash — lash mapping](https://londonlashpro.com/blogs/news/the-ultimate-lash-mapping-guide-for-lash-techs) |

These are visual style references, not a validated clinical comparison, guaranteed
results, application instructions or evidence of Ellen's training. Actual density
and styling depend on the person's natural lashes and professional assessment.
Prices, final services and availability remain explicitly unconfirmed.

## Asset brief and integration

Use the built-in image generation tool, one original photo-style image per look.
Every frame is landscape 3:2 with a single open adult eye, its complete upper-lash
fringe and both corners visible. Avoid full faces, nose, mouth, hair, hands, tools,
makeup, logos, captions or copied reference photography. Use comparable light,
natural skin texture and a hazel-brown eye so differences primarily come from the
lash shape and density. The full-volume example must be visibly much denser than
the natural lift; the cat-eye example must emphasize the outer sweep.

The selected assets are optimized locally as 960 × 640 WebP images under
`ellen-studio/catalogue/`. Images are presented as AI-generated illustrative
references, never client work, actual treatment photographs or an image of Ellen.
The gallery and image viewer preserve the full 3:2 frame with `object-fit: contain`.
No original user photograph is edited in this change.

The exact five built-in generation prompts, final repository paths and inspection
notes are preserved in [ellen-studio-lash-prompts.json](ellen-studio-lash-prompts.json).
